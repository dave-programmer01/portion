How does photo food logging work?

Tap the + button and snap your meal. The entry appears instantly as "analyzing" while our AI reads the plate and fills in the foods, calories, and macros. If a scan can't be read, it's marked failed, it costs you nothing, and you can retry or log it manually. You can also add food by scanning a barcode or searching our food database.

How accurate are the calorie numbers?

AI photo estimates are best-effort and depend on what the camera can see, so treat them as a close starting point rather than an exact measurement. You can edit any item, remove foods or adjust portions, before or after logging. Barcode and database entries use published nutrition data.

How are my calorie and macro targets calculated?

Your daily targets come from the Mifflin-St Jeor formula using your profile (age, sex, height, weight, activity level) and your goal, not from AI. They recalculate whenever you update your profile or log a new weight.

How do workout plans work?

After onboarding, Portion builds a weekly plan from a curated, beginner-friendly exercise library based on your goal, experience, equipment, and available days. Open a day to see the exercises, sets, and reps, then log your sets as you go. A rest timer starts automatically between sets.

Can I save a meal to log again later?

Yes. On any logged meal in your Food diary, tap the star icon to save it as a favorite. Saved meals can be re-logged in one tap from the log screen, handy for meals you eat often.

What's the difference between Free and Premium?

The free tier includes a set number of AI photo scans per day, a recent history window, and a starter workout plan. Premium unlocks unlimited photo scans, full history, and additional workout options such as more training days and plan regeneration. Barcode scanning, search, and manual logging are always free.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Manage, upgrade, or cancel anytime from your store account settings, if you cancel, Premium stays active until the end of the current billing period. To restore a purchase on a new device, use Restore purchases on the paywall screen.

How do I change units or language?

Open Settings → Preferences. Tap Units to switch between metric (kg, cm) and imperial (lb, ft). Portion follows your device language; you can change that in your device's system settings.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently erases your profile, food and workout logs, saved meals, weight history, and meal photos, and signs you out. This can't be undone.
