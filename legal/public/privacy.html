This policy describes how the Portion app actually handles your data. It is provided for transparency and is not legal advice.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how David Opabunmi ("Portion", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use the Portion mobile application for iOS and Android (the "App") and related services (together, the "Service").

Portion is an AI-assisted calorie-tracking and workout-planning app. To provide it, we necessarily process information about your body, your meals, and your training — some of which is considered health-related and sensitive. Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Service.

2. Who we are

Portion is operated by David Opabunmi, an independent individual developer based in New York, USA ("we", "us", or "our"), who is the data controller responsible for your information. For any privacy question or request, contact us at opabunmidavid@gmail.com.

3. Data we collect

We collect the following categories of data. We only collect what the App actually uses to work.

Account & identity

When you sign in with Apple or Google through our authentication provider (Clerk), we receive and store your email address, first and last name, and profile image, and a unique account identifier.

Health & fitness profile (sensitive data)

During onboarding you provide information used to calculate your targets and build your plan, including your goal, sex, age, height, weight, target weight, activity level, training experience, available equipment, training days per week, and any injuries you enter as free text. We also ask health-screening questions and store a health flag indicating whether you told us you are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a medical condition such as a heart condition, diabetes, or an eating disorder. This is sensitive/special-category data, processed based on your explicit consent (see Section 5).

Food logs & meal photos

When you log food, we store the meal entries, individual food items, quantities, calories and macronutrients, meal type, notes, and dates. If you photo-log a meal, we store the photo (hosted by our image provider, ImageKit) and the AI's estimate of its contents. We also store any saved meals ("favorites") you create.

Workouts & body metrics

We store your generated workout plans, logged training sessions and sets (including reps and weights lifted), and your body-weight history over time.

Barcode & food-search queries

When you scan a barcode or search for a food, the barcode number or search term is sent to Open Food Facts to look up nutrition data.

Subscription & purchase data

If you subscribe, our in-app-purchase provider (RevenueCat, via the Apple App Store or Google Play) tells us your subscription status and expiry. Payment is processed by Apple or Google — we never receive your full card or payment details.

Technical & diagnostic data

To keep the App reliable, our monitoring provider (Sentry) collects crash reports, error logs, performance traces, device and app information, and masked session-replay recordings (on-screen text and images are masked — see Section 7). Our product-analytics provider (PostHog) collects a limited set of product events — such as completing onboarding, logging food, viewing the paywall, and starting a subscription — along with a device/app identifier, to help us understand which features are used and where people get stuck. These analytics events never include your health data, meal photos, or the contents of your food logs. We also keep an internal log of AI requests (model, token counts, cost, latency, and success) to control costs.

4. How we collect it

  • Directly from you — onboarding answers, food and workout logs, weight entries, photos, and notes.
  • Automatically — diagnostic, performance, and session-replay data as you use the App.
  • From third parties — your name, email, and profile image from Apple or Google when you sign in; your subscription status from RevenueCat / the app stores.

5. How we use your data (and our legal bases)

We use your data to:

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate you — performance of a contract.
  • Calculate your calorie and macronutrient targets (using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula, not AI) and generate your workout plan — performance of a contract; and, for health-related profile data, your explicit consent.
  • Estimate the contents of meal photos and log your food and workouts — performance of a contract / explicit consent for health data.
  • Provide, maintain, secure, debug, and improve the Service — our legitimate interests in operating a reliable product.
  • Process subscriptions and enforce plan limits — performance of a contract.
  • Communicate with you about the Service and respond to support requests — legitimate interests / contract.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms — legal obligation / legitimate interests.

Where we rely on consent (for example, to process your health data), you can withdraw it at any time by deleting the relevant data or your account; this will not affect processing done before withdrawal. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

6. AI & automated processing

Two features use a third-party AI model. During the current beta, that model is OpenAI's gpt-4o-mini, called only from our secure back-end (never from your device):

  • Meal photo analysis — your meal photo (downsized) is sent to OpenAI to estimate the foods and their macros.
  • Workout generation — your goal, experience, equipment, training days, and the injuries text you entered are sent to OpenAI to build a plan from our curated exercise library.

AI outputs are best-effort estimates and may be inaccurate. They are not medical, nutritional, or professional advice. We do not use these features to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you without human involvement. OpenAI processes this data as our service provider; under its API terms, data submitted through the API is not used to train OpenAI's models.

Confirmed, de-identified nutrition facts (food name, brand, and macros — not your identity or photo) may be added to a shared food database to improve lookups for everyone.

7. Diagnostics, analytics & session replay

We use Sentry for crash reporting, performance monitoring, and structured logs, and PostHog for product analytics. PostHog receives only the specific product events described in Section 3; it does not automatically capture your taps or screen contents, and we do not use PostHog session replay. Product analytics are off by default and opt-in: we ask for your consent, nothing is collected until you agree, and you can turn it on or off anytime in Settings → Share usage data.

Sentry also provides session replay, which records a reconstruction of your screens to help us diagnose problems. We configure it to mask all on-screen text, images, and graphics, so replays capture layout and interaction patterns rather than the personal or health information you view or enter. We also do not attach your IP address to diagnostic events by default.

8. Sharing & sub-processors

We share data with the service providers below so they can perform functions on our behalf. We do not sell your data or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

  • Clerk — authentication and identity (name, email, profile image).
  • Neon — our primary database, hosting your account, profile, food, workout, and weight data.
  • OpenAI — AI meal-photo and workout processing (meal photos; workout inputs including injuries text).
  • ImageKit — storage, resizing, and delivery of meal photos.
  • Open Food Facts — barcode and food-name lookups (receives your barcode / search term).
  • RevenueCat, and the Apple App Store / Google Play — subscription management and payment processing.
  • Sentry — diagnostics, performance monitoring, and (masked) session replay.
  • PostHog — product analytics (the limited product events in Section 3, plus a device/app identifier).
  • Inngest — background job orchestration (processes account identifiers and image references to run the AI jobs).

Each provider processes data on our behalf under its own terms and privacy policy. We may also disclose data where required by law, to enforce our terms, or in connection with a sale or transfer of the Service (with notice where required).

9. International transfers

We are based in the United States, and we and our providers may process your data there and in other countries. Where personal data is transferred out of the EEA, UK, or other regulated regions, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Retention & deletion

We keep your account and in-app data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from the App's Settings; doing so removes your account and all associated records from our database and deletes your identity from our authentication provider.

When you delete your account, your meal photos are also deleted from ImageKit along with your account and records. Some data held by our providers is handled under their own terms:

  • Diagnostic data held by Sentry is kept under its default retention (generally up to 90 days) and then removed.
  • Data sent to OpenAI's API is retained by OpenAI for a limited period (up to 30 days) for abuse monitoring, then deleted, and is not used to train its models.
  • Subscription records held by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat are retained under their policies and any legal or tax obligations.

In general, we keep your data only while your account is active. When you delete your account it is removed promptly from our active systems, and any residual copies in routine backups are cycled out shortly afterward.

11. Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect your data, including: encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS); authentication tokens stored in your device's secure storage (Keychain / Keystore); short-lived, signed credentials for photo uploads so our storage keys never reach your device; authenticated and verified webhooks; and access controls that ensure every request is scoped to your own account. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights:

  • Access a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectify inaccurate data (you can edit most profile data in the App).
  • Erase your data (delete your account in Settings).
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent for health-data processing at any time.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a portable format.
  • Lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

EEA/UK users (GDPR / UK GDPR): the legal bases above apply and you may contact your supervisory authority. California users (CCPA/CPRA): you have rights to know, delete, and correct your personal information, and to not be discriminated against for exercising them; we do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined.

Note: some rights (such as full access/portability export) are not yet available as a self-service feature in the App; until they are, we will fulfil verified requests manually. To make a request, email opabunmidavid@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

13. Children

Portion is not intended for children. You must be at least 16 years old to use the Service. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16; if you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App. Your continued use of the Service after an update means you accept the revised policy.
