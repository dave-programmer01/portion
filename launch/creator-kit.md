# Creator / UGC outreach kit (free-Premium offer)

Goal: get **5–10 authentic "watch me log my food" posts** live from micro-creators
(5k–50k followers) in fitness / "what I eat in a day" / student-fitness / weight-loss
niches. Cast wide — a free-Premium-only offer converts lower, so **DM 20–30 to land
5–10**. Spread across your target geographies (global + Nigeria/Africa where the big
apps are weak).

Attribution is built in: each creator shares **their in-app referral code**, so every
signup is tied to them (see per-creator query in `launch/posthog-funnel.md`).

---

## 1. Outreach DM  (short, personal, not corporate)

> Hey [name] — really like your [specific thing, e.g. "high-protein on a budget"] content.
>
> I built an app called Portion: you snap a photo of your food and it logs the
> calories in seconds, then tells you what to eat and how to train that day. Made it
> because every calorie app just gives you an empty diary.
>
> I'd love to give you **free Premium (on the house, no strings)** to try it. If you
> like it, a genuine "watch me log my meals" clip would mean a lot — and you'd get a
> code where you and your followers both get a free week.
>
> Cool if I send you a TestFlight link? 🙌

Keep it 1:1. Reference something real from their feed in the first line. No pitch deck.

## 2. One-page creator brief  (send after they say yes)

**What it is:** Portion — snap a photo of your meal → instant calories + macros, plus
a daily "what to eat / how to train" plan. Beginner-friendly, built for global food.

**What we're asking:** one honest short-form video (Reel / TikTok / Short, 20–45s)
showing you actually using it. Authentic > polished. Not an ad read.

**The offer:**
- Free Premium for you (lifetime for our first creators).
- Your referral code: you + anyone who joins with it each get a free week of Premium.
- (Optional) we'll repost/duet your clip.

**The one thing to show:** the *magic moment* — point the camera at your food and the
calories appear. That's the hook.

**Please include:**
- Your referral code on screen + in caption (that's how we say thanks + track it).
- A line that it "tells you what to eat next," not just the number.

**Timeline:** post within ~1 week of getting access. Send us the link when it's live.

**Don't:** overclaim accuracy or make medical claims. "Estimates that get you close in
seconds" is the honest, allowed framing.

## 3. Loose content script  (guide, not a teleprompter)

**Hook (0–3s):** "I stopped guessing my calories — watch this."
→ Point phone at your plate.

**Magic moment (3–12s):** Snap. Show the result card: "It just told me this is ~X
calories and Y protein. That fast."

**The twist (12–25s):** "But the part I actually like — it tells me what to eat next
to hit my protein, and gives me today's workout. It's like a coach, not a food diary."

**Proof / relatability (25–35s):** Show logging your real, normal food (bonus: a local
dish most apps can't handle). "It even gets [jollof / my mum's cooking / whatever]."

**CTA (35–45s):** "It's called Portion. Use my code [CODE] — we both get a free week of
Premium. Link in bio."

**Caption template:**
> Snapping my food instead of guessing 📸 @ portionapp
> Use code [CODE] — we both get a free week of Premium 🎁
> #caloriecounter #whatieatinaday #fitnessjourney #[localtag]
